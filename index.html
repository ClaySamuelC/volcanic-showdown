How to play

Controls

  • RMBMove. Your hero turns toward where it is walking.
  • LMBBasic attack toward the cursor. Hold to repeat.
  • QWEAbilities, aimed at the cursor.
  • RUltimate. Starts each round on cooldown.
  • SStop moving.
  • AltHold over an ability for full details.
  • EscOptions.
  • MToggle music.

The arena

  • A health pack with health appears in the middle every s. The last hit heals .
  • powder kegs appear every s and the previous ones disappear. They have health and explode s after they break.
  • Rocks have health. Broken ones return and damaged ones heal every s. Four pillars stand in a square around the center and never move.
  • After seconds the lava closes in. Standing in it burns health per second.
  • A round ends on death, or after s to the hero with more health. First to rounds wins the match.
