Volcanic Showdown

Isometric 1v1 arena. Best of 7. Last hero standing takes the round.

Multiplayer

How to play
  • Right-click to move. The hero turns toward where it is walking.
  • Left click fires your basic attack toward the cursor.
  • Q / W / E basic abilities, R ultimate (starts on cooldown).
  • Esc opens options. M toggles the music.
  • A health pack with 5 health appears in the middle every 20s. The last hit heals 4.
  • Two powder kegs appear every 20s, and the previous ones disappear. They have 2 health and explode 0.5s after they break.
  • Rocks have 6 health. Broken ones return, and damaged ones heal, every 20s. Four pillars stand in a square around the center and stay there.
  • After 45s the lava closes in.
  • Rounds end on death or at 2 minutes (higher health wins). First to 4 rounds wins the match.
