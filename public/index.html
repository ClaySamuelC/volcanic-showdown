Volcanic Showdown

Isometric 1v1 arena. Best of 7. Last hero standing takes the round.

Multiplayer

Multiplayer server

Practice mode runs entirely in your browser. Multiplayer lobbies need the Node server from this repo hosted somewhere reachable by both players.

How to play
  • Right-click or arrow keys to move. Mouse aims everything.
  • Left click fires your basic attack toward the cursor.
  • Q / W / E basic abilities, R ultimate (starts on cooldown).
  • 12 health each. Health packs heal 3. After 45s the lava closes in.
  • Rounds end on death or at 2 minutes (higher health wins). First to 4 rounds wins the match.
